## What is Tayyab Shawls?

An online store for shawls — lawn, Swiss lawn, cotton, handwork, Irani, embroidered, fancy and party wear.

- You browse the **categories**.
- You add shawls to the **cart**.
- You pay on delivery, JazzCash or Easypaisa.
- **Rs. 200 shipping all over Pakistan**, Cash on Delivery available.

**In one line:** pick a shawls, order it, it reaches your door.

## Features

- **Hero Slider** - Auto-sliding banners for Bridal Elegance, New Season Arrivals and Formal & Party Wear.
- **Shop by Category** - Lawn Cutwork, Lawn, Irani, Embroidered, Printed, Party Wear, Fancy and Handwork shawls in one grid.
- **Best Sellers** - Driven by real sales count in the database, with a manual admin override.
- **New Arrivals** - Anything uploaded in the last 7 days shows up automatically and expires by itself.
- **Shop Page** - Category checkbox tree, price range filter, sort by newest/oldest/price, 48 products per page.
- **Sale Page** - Only discounted products, paginated, with an empty state when nothing is on sale.
- **Cart & Checkout** - Mini-cart drawer, full cart, coupons, and checkout with JazzCash / Easypaisa / COD.
- **Wishlist & Reviews** - Save favourites, leave reviews (approved from the admin panel).
- **Customer Accounts** - Email/password login plus Continue with Google and TikTok.
- **Search & Newsletter** - Product search and a newsletter signup in the footer.
- **WhatsApp Support** - Chat widget and direct WhatsApp link (0331-4863100).
- **Admin Panel** - Dashboard with revenue chart, products CRUD, bulk CSV import (with image download), 3-level categories, orders, customers, coupons, reviews, hero banners and settings.
- **SEO** - Metadata, canonical, Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and slug-based URLs.

## Why Tayyab Shawls?

- **Real categories** - lawn, Swiss lawn, cotton, handwork, Irani, embroidered, fancy and party wear, not one messy grid.
- **New Arrivals update themselves** - products uploaded in the last 7 days appear on their own.
- **Best Sellers follow sales** - the more it sells, the higher it ranks.
- **PKR pricing** - local prices for Pakistan, Rs. 200 flat shipping.
- **Cash on Delivery** - plus JazzCash and Easypaisa for people who prefer online payment.
- **Full admin panel** - import products from CSV with images, manage orders, coupons, banners and settings.
